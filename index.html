Music Channel Tools

Private management tools for the YouTube channel "The Music Channel".

Music Channel Tools is a small, private application used only by the owner of "The Music Channel" on YouTube. It connects to the owner's own YouTube account to:

  • keep the channel's 24/7 music radio live stream running, and restart its YouTube broadcast if it stops;
  • read the channel's own video statistics and analytics;
  • update the channel's own video titles, descriptions, thumbnails and playlists.

It is not a public service and does not access any other user's account or data.
